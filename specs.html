A drill program without the SRU, and with it

What changes in mud handling at the drill when the SRU is on the pad.

ItemWithout the SRUWith the MDS SRU
Drilling mudDumped once it is loaded with cuttings, then new mud is mixedSolids are taken out at the drill and the same mud goes back down the hole
Mud pails flown inEvery batch that is dumped has to be replacedFewer. Reused mud only needs topping up.
WaterReplace the fluid that is dumpedLess to pump or haul. Recycled fluid is used again.
SumpTakes the dumped fluid and the cuttingsTakes the separated solids, with less fluid to contain
Reclamation and haul-outMore volume to reclaim or haul out at the end of the programLess volume to reclaim or haul out
Helicopter tripsRegular resupply runs for mud and waterFewer resupply runs for mud and water
FuelFuel burned on those resupply flightsFewer flights. The SRU’s own generator has low fuel consumption.
Mixing on the padMixed separately at the drillPremix tank with high-shear mixer built into the SRU

This compares mud handling at a diamond drill with and without solids removal. Actual results depend on ground conditions, the drilling program and site requirements.
