05With and without the SRU

What changes when the SRU is on the pad

Same drill, same program. The SRU cleans the mud at the drill so it goes back down the hole instead of into the sump.

What changes Without the SRU With the MDS SRU
Drilling mud Dumped when it fills with cuttings Cleaned at the drill and reused
Mud pails flown in Every dumped batch is replaced Fewer. Reused mud only needs topping up.
Water Replace every load that is dumped Less to pump or haul
Sump Holds the dumped fluid and the cuttings Holds the separated solids, with less fluid
Reclamation and haul-out More volume at the end of the program Less volume to reclaim or haul out
Helicopter trips Regular mud and water resupply Fewer resupply runs
Fuel Burned on every resupply flight Fewer flights, and a low-fuel generator
Mixing on the pad Mixed separately at the drill Built-in premix tank and high-shear mixer
