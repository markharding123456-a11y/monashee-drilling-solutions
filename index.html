01The SRU

Solids removal, polymer mixing and power on one aluminum frame

The MDS SRU is a purpose-built solids removal unit for diamond drilling operations. It takes the solids out of the drilling fluid so the mud can be recycled. It carries its own 12 kW Kubota generator; no auxiliary generator is needed.
