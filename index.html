01What it does

What it does, in plain terms

A diamond drill pumps drilling fluid, or mud, down the hole to cool the bit and carry the rock cuttings back up. That mud is expensive. The SRU takes the solids out of it right at the drill, so the same mud can go back down the hole instead of being dumped.

  1. Step 1

    Mix

    New polymer mud is pre-mixed in the SRU’s own tank with a high-shear mixer.

  2. Step 2

    Drill

    The mud goes down the hole and comes back up carrying rock cuttings.

  3. Step 3

    Clean

    The SRU’s solids removal system takes the cuttings out of the fluid.

  4. Step 4

    Reuse

    The cleaned mud goes back to the drill to be used again.

All of it runs off the unit’s own 12 kW Kubota generator. There is no separate generator to fly in or hook up.
